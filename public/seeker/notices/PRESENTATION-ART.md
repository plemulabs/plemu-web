# Game presentation artwork

The cover and banner textures use credited author-published artwork, native emulator
captures of the exact reviewed game binaries, and explicitly identified Plemu AI-assisted
presentation illustrations. The new Libbet, Rayslinger and Lunar Limit covers are Plemu
illustrations guided by their original authors' artwork/sprites; they are not official box
art, new gameplay screenshots or changes to the games. Quartet uses its author's original
flat label from the supplied archive. Author credits, source URLs, exact artwork digests
and transformation records are in PRESENTATION-ART-SOURCES.json. Game credits and original
license notices remain unchanged. Standard/pack placement does not imply a CC0 license.
